Adaptation & Intervention in an Academic Institution

The Bishop’s School, Camp, Pune

Institutional · Thesis · 2025

Architectural thesis. Redevelopment of a 160-year-old campus, an institution I was part of for fourteen years. A demolition-value analysis weighed the sentimental, structural, associative, historical and functional worth of the existing built mass; the demolished Harding Hall lives on through its random rubble masonry, reused within the new built form.
